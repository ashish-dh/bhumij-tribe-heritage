Demographic & Vulnerability Overview

Current Status, Population & Conservation Focus

Constitutional Status

Classified as a Scheduled Tribe (ST) in Jharkhand, West Bengal, Odisha, and Tripura. In Assam tea estate belts, community councils press for constitutional ST recognition.

Regional Concentration

Concentrated in East & West Singhbhum (Dhalbhum, Potka), Seraikela, Manbhum, and Ranchi in Jharkhand; Jhargram, Purulia, Bankura in WB; and Mayurbhanj in Odisha.
